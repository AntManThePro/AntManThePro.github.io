Section 1 · The Foundation
The Only 3 Things That Matter
1

Blue before you clean. Document everything BEFORE you touch it.

2

Green after you finish. Document everything AFTER it is clean.

3

Red only if there is a problem. Missing blue or green means you cannot check out.

Section 2 · Status Codes
Job Status — What They Mean
On the Way.
Driving. No work. No photos.
Arrived Onsite.
You are there. Do not start yet.
Waiting on Access.
Cannot start. Use comments. Wide photo if blocked.
Check In. Work Starts Here.
Take BLUE docs. Call office.
Check Out. Work Ends Here.
Cleaning done. GREEN docs. Metadata complete. Check out with the control tower BEFORE locking the location and setting the alarm.
Needs SM Review.
After checkout, finish getting ready to leave the jobsite, then change status from Check Out to Needs SM Review. QA takes over.
Section 3 · Before Cleaning
Blue — Kitchen and Hood
Before Cleaning
  • Hood walkthrough video.
  • Pilot lights video. Flames must be visible.
  • Fire pull station tag. Front and back.
  • Wide view of appliances under hood.
  • Slow appliance line video. Left to right, about 5 feet back.
  • Hood with filters IN.
  • Hood with filters OUT.
  • Upper and lower tracks.
  • Plenum and detection line. Wide shot.
  • Backsplash and behind appliances.
  • Work area and return vents.
  • All duct collars.
Section 3 · Before Cleaning
Blue — Duct and Access Panels
Before Cleaning
  • Overall duct view.
  • Location of each panel.
  • Panel ON. Sticker must be visible.
  • Panel OFF. Outside the hole.
  • View toward hood.
  • View toward vertical duct.
  • Fire wrap condition.

No panel exists? Mark N/A Not Present. Never leave blank. Never guess. This is the most audited item.

Blue — Rooftop and Fan
Before Cleaning

Section 4 · After Cleaning
Green — Same Areas. Clean Condition.
After Cleaning

Everything you shot in Blue must be verified in Green.

If QA cannot see it clean, it does not count.

Section 5 · Technique
How to Shoot Video
Prevents 80 percent of QA fails
  • Stand far enough back to see the whole area.
  • Move slow and steady.
  • One continuous shot. Do not stop and restart.
  • Pause 2 to 3 seconds on important areas.
NEVER DO THIS

Do not zoom in. Do not jump between sections. Do not shoot too close. If you cannot see the full component, you are too close.

Section 5 · Pro Tip
Grease Removal. Key Fact.
Important

Heavy grease buildup breaks down with hot water and degreaser. Cold water makes grease solidify and re-adhere to surfaces, making your job harder. Apply degreaser, let it dwell, then rinse with hot water from the top down.

Section 6 · Deficiencies
Red — When to Use It
Problems Only
  • You could not reach something.
  • Something is damaged.
  • Something is missing.
  • Fire wrap looks compromised.
  • Access is blocked.

Red must match one real issue. Red must be clear. Red is not a failure. Hiding it is.

Section 8 · Checkout
Metadata Required to Check Out
Confirm all before checkout
System type.
Duct orientation.
Fan type.
Stories.
Access panel status.
Missing metadata means blocked checkout. No exceptions.
Section 8 · Critical Reminder
Access Panel Rule.
Reminder

If a panel does not exist at the site, mark it N/A – Not Present. Never leave the field blank. Never guess. A blank or guessed entry is an automatic QA flag. This is the most audited item on the job.

Section 9 · Watch Out
Common QA Blocks
Avoid these every job
  • Missing pilot lights video.
  • Filters out plenum not shown.
  • Appliance video too close.
  • Panel not shown or marked N/A.
  • Red issue uploaded with no explanation.
  • Photos in wrong bucket. Before or after.
Section 11 · Before You Leave
Quick Self Check Before Checkout
Did I shoot Blue before cleaning?
Did I verify every Blue area in Green?
Did I mark access limits clearly?
Did I complete all metadata?
Did I use Red only where needed?
Did I check out with the control tower BEFORE locking the location and setting the alarm?
Did I change status from Check Out to Needs SM Review before leaving?
All yes means you are done and checkout will pass.
The Standard
The Final Rule

If QA can recreate the job using your photos and videos, you did it right. That is the standard.
