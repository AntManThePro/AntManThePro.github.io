AI AUTO-DETECT — HONEST STATUS

Automatic AI detection of a hood or duct length from a photo alone is NOT in this build, on purpose. Without a known scale, camera angle and depth, an AI guess in feet would be invented data, and this is a compliance / bidding tool. The next step for true auto-detect is a vision API (with its own key, kept out of the app file) plus this same calibration step — and even then, tape-verify it. What IS in this build: on-device metadata, calibrated tap-to-measure, and a measurement list you can push straight into Measured Duct Pieces. If you want, you can also drop a photo in this chat and I can describe what is in it and read any visible labels — that is a separate, human-checked step, not an automatic measurement.
